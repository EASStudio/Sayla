User: What is a prime number?
Assistant: A prime number is a whole number greater than 1 that has no divisors other than 1 and itself. For example, 7 is prime because nothing between 2 and 6 divides it evenly.

User: What is the derivative of x squared?
Assistant: The derivative of x squared is 2x. In general, the derivative of x raised to the power n is n times x raised to the power n minus 1.

User: How do you solve a linear equation like 2x + 3 = 11?
Assistant: Subtract 3 from both sides to get 2x = 8, then divide both sides by 2 to get x = 4.

User: What is the Pythagorean theorem?
Assistant: For a right triangle with legs a and b and hypotenuse c, a squared plus b squared equals c squared.

User: What is a fraction?
Assistant: A fraction represents a part of a whole, written as a numerator over a denominator, like 3/4 meaning 3 parts out of 4 equal parts.

User: How do you add fractions with different denominators?
Assistant: Find a common denominator, convert both fractions to that denominator, then add the numerators and keep the denominator the same.

User: What is the area of a circle?
Assistant: The area of a circle is pi times the radius squared, written as A = pi r^2.

User: What is a factorial?
Assistant: A factorial of a number n, written n!, is the product of every whole number from 1 up to n. For example, 5! = 5 x 4 x 3 x 2 x 1 = 120.

User: What does it mean for two lines to be parallel?
Assistant: Two lines are parallel if they lie in the same plane and never intersect, no matter how far they're extended.

User: What is the quadratic formula?
Assistant: For an equation ax^2 + bx + c = 0, x equals negative b plus or minus the square root of b squared minus 4ac, all divided by 2a.

User: What is a matrix?
Assistant: A matrix is a rectangular grid of numbers arranged in rows and columns, often used to represent linear transformations or systems of equations.

User: What is the difference between mean and median?
Assistant: The mean is the sum of all values divided by how many there are. The median is the middle value when all values are sorted in order.

User: What is an integral?
Assistant: An integral finds the area under a curve, or more generally, accumulates a quantity that's changing continuously, and is the reverse operation of a derivative.

User: What is the greatest common divisor?
Assistant: The greatest common divisor of two numbers is the largest number that divides both of them evenly.

User: How do you find the slope of a line?
Assistant: The slope is the change in y divided by the change in x between two points on the line, often written as rise over run.

User: What is a polynomial?
Assistant: A polynomial is an expression made up of variables raised to whole-number powers, combined with addition, subtraction, and multiplication, like 3x^2 + 2x - 5.

User: What does it mean for a number to be a perfect square?
Assistant: A perfect square is a number that results from multiplying an integer by itself, like 16, which is 4 times 4.

User: What is probability?
Assistant: Probability measures how likely an event is to happen, expressed as a number between 0 and 1, where 0 means impossible and 1 means certain.

User: What is the order of operations?
Assistant: The order of operations tells you to solve parentheses first, then exponents, then multiplication and division left to right, then addition and subtraction left to right.

User: What is an absolute value?
Assistant: The absolute value of a number is its distance from zero on the number line, always written as a non-negative number, like |-5| = 5.
